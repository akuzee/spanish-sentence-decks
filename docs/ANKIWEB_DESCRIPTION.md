```
The best Spanish sentence decks on AnkiWeb (the two Tatoeba decks, Neri's Spanish Sentences), merged into one and sorted from easy to hard. Duplicates, fragments and junk removed; every word inside the 5,000 most frequent (up to B2). 13,615 sentences, English translations, audio on 7,810 (native speakers where available).

PAIRED WITH MY VOCAB DECK
"Ultimate top 5000 spanish vocab - best decks merged, customizable to your level" uses the same frequency list. By sentence 1,000 you should know its first ~700 words; by 5,000, ~1,850; by 10,000, ~3,100.

CUSTOMIZE IT TO YOUR LEVEL

Rough guide: A2 delete 1–2 / review 3–4; B1 delete 1–4 / review 5–6; B2 delete 1–6 / review 7–8. Also tagged: grammar (gram::subj, gram::past, …), Spain-specific sentences (region::*), source and audio.

CARDS
Read (Spanish → English, hardest word highlighted) and Translate (English → Spanish, with optional hints). Rarer vocabulary is in the companion deck "… (extra 4,701 challenge sentences)".

Level guide, how it was built, more versions, and a 78,000-sentence bank you can have an LLM pull extra practice from: https://github.com/akuzee/spanish-sentence-decks

## 3. Vocabulary deck

File: `decks/Ultimate top 5000 spanish vocab - best decks merged, customizable to your level.apkg`
Title: `Ultimate top 5000 spanish vocab - best decks merged, customizable to your level`
Tags: `spanish vocabulary audio pictures frequency`

```
The two best "Top 5000" Spanish vocabulary decks on AnkiWeb ("Essential Spanish Vocabulary Top 5000", "New Spanish Top 5000 with Examples"), merged into one: every word gets the picture from the first and the two example sentences and natural-sounding audio from the second. 5,601 words in frequency order, English meanings.

PAIRED WITH MY SENTENCE DECK
"Ultimate top 20000 spanish sentences - best decks merged, customizable to your level" is sorted on the same frequency list. Knowing the first ~700 words gets you through its first 1,000 sentences; ~1,850 words → 5,000 sentences; ~3,100 → 10,000.

CUSTOMIZE IT TO YOUR LEVEL
Words are tagged by frequency band (rank::0001-0500 … rank::4501-5000) and cognates by cognate::strong / cognate::weak. Search a band in the browser and delete it, or make it occasional reviews with Cards → Set Due Date → "90-180!". Rough guide: A2 review ≤500; B1 delete ≤1000, review 1001–2000; B2 delete ≤2000, review 2001–3000. Also tagged: part of speech (pos::nm, pos::v, …).

CARDS
Spanish → English (word and audio on the front; meaning, picture and examples on the back) and English → Spanish (meaning and picture on the front).

Level guide and details: https://github.com/akuzee/spanish-sentence-decks

Buy me a coffee if it helped: https://buymeacoffee.com/akuzee

Credits: built from the AnkiWeb decks "Essential Spanish Vocabulary Top 5000" and "New Spanish Top 5000 with Examples"; word list and glosses follow Mark Davies' A Frequency Dictionary of Spanish.
