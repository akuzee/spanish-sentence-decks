# AnkiWeb description (main deck)

Deck title: **Ultimate top 20000 spanish sentences - best decks merged, customizable to your level**

---

The best Spanish sentence decks on AnkiWeb, combined into one and sorted from easy to hard.

13,615 sentences with English translations: every usable sentence from the two Tatoeba decks, plus lines from
Neri's Spanish Sentences wherever they add a word or structure Tatoeba lacks. De-duplicated, fragments and broken
lines removed, the beginner end trimmed so you move on when you should, every word inside the 5,000 most frequent.
Audio on 7,810 (57%): native recordings where they exist, a synthetic voice otherwise. Finish it and you have seen
every common word in 5–12 sentences and every grammar pattern at every level; after that, real content beats more
sentence drilling. At 20–50 new cards a day it is a year or two of practice; suspend the Translate cards
(search card:Translate) to halve it. Everything here uses
vocabulary inside the 5,000 most frequent words, i.e. up to B2; sentences that need rarer words are a separate
deck, "Ultimate top 20000 spanish sentences - best decks merged, customizable to your level (extra 4,701 challenge sentences)". Every sentence is scored for difficulty (how rare its hardest word is, plus grammar like
subjunctive or conditional), so the deck starts with "Es lo que es" and ends with the subjunctive and rare
vocabulary.

TWO CARDS PER SENTENCE
• Read: Spanish → English. The hardest word is highlighted with its meaning.
• Translate: English → the full Spanish sentence. Two optional hints (the sentence with that word blanked, and
  the word itself) for when a sentence is new to you.

PAIR IT WITH THE VOCAB DECK
Same frequency list, so you can pace them together ("Ultimate top 5000 spanish vocab - best decks merged, customizable to your level"):
sentence 500 → know the first ~330 words · 1,000 → ~720 · 2,500 → ~1,400 · 5,000 → ~1,850 · 10,000 → ~3,100 · end → ~4,200.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Start at your level: every card has a level::01–10 tag. Delete the levels below you, or turn them into
  occasional reviews with Cards → Set Due Date → "90-180!". Presets for A2/B1/B2 are in the guide below.
• Latin American Spanish: Spain-specific sentences (vosotros, Spain-only words) are tagged region::* – search
  tag:region::* and suspend them.
• Grammar drills: tag:gram::subj, gram::cond, gram::perf, gram::past, gram::fut, gram::imper, gram::ger.
• Audio on demand: deck options → "Don't play audio automatically".
• Need more at your level, or more subjunctive, or only audio sentences? The whole 78,000-sentence bank is on
  GitHub with instructions you can paste to Claude/ChatGPT to build an add-on matched to what you struggle with.

Level guide, method and other versions (everything-included, Latin American, B1 preset, with TTS audio):
https://github.com/akuzee/spanish-sentence-decks

# Vocabulary deck

Deck title: **Ultimate top 5000 spanish vocab - best decks merged, customizable to your level**

The two best "Top 5000" Spanish vocabulary decks on AnkiWeb, merged into one: the pictures from *Essential
Spanish Vocabulary Top 5000* and the example sentences and natural-sounding audio from *New Spanish Top 5000
with Examples*. 5,601 words, matched by headword (the two decks use different frequency lists, so about 600 words
come from only one of them), ordered by frequency.

TWO CARDS PER WORD
• Spanish → English: word and audio on the front; meaning, picture and two example sentences (with translations)
  on the back.
• English → Spanish: meaning and picture on the front; word, audio and examples on the back.

PAIR IT WITH THE SENTENCE DECK
"Ultimate top 20000 spanish sentences - best decks merged, customizable to your level" is sorted on the same list: when you know the first ~720 words you are ready for its first 1,000 sentences;
~1,850 words → 5,000 sentences; ~3,100 words → 10,000; ~4,200 → the whole deck.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Start at your level: words are tagged by frequency band (rank::0001-0500 … rank::4501-5000). Delete the bands
  below you, or turn them into occasional reviews with Cards → Set Due Date → "90-180!". Suggested: A2 review
  rank ≤500; B1 delete ≤1000, review 1001–2000; B2 delete ≤2000, review 2001–3000.
• Cognates (words that look like their English translation) are tagged cognate::strong / cognate::weak. The
  Spanish → English card of a cognate is usually free; keep the English → Spanish card.
• Part of speech tags: pos::nm, pos::nf, pos::v, pos::adj, …
